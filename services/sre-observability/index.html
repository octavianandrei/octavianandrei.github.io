SRE & Observability Consulting

I help teams understand service health and turn operational signals into useful decisions. SRE and observability work connects metrics, logs, and traces with the user journeys that matter, then defines how the team detects and responds to problems.

When this work helps

More dashboards do not automatically mean better reliability. Noisy alerts, missing context, and unclear ownership can leave a team reacting to symptoms. A reliability review identifies the signals worth collecting and the procedures needed to act on them.

What we can work on

  • Identify critical user journeys and define suitable service level indicators and objectives.
  • Instrument services and telemetry collection with OpenTelemetry.
  • Connect metrics, logs, and traces with dashboards in tools such as Prometheus and Grafana.
  • Review alert thresholds, routing, ownership, and incident response procedures.
  • Create runbooks and review opportunities to reduce recurring operational work.

What the handover can include

The agreed scope can include instrumentation configuration, dashboards, alert rules, service objective definitions, and incident runbooks. Validation focuses on whether the team can detect a relevant failure, locate the affected service, and follow the response procedure.

A useful starting point

Bring your architecture, monitoring tools, recurring incidents, and the alerts your team finds most difficult to act on.

We start by discussing your current setup, constraints, and intended outcome. From there, we agree on a focused assessment, a defined project, or ongoing engineering support.
