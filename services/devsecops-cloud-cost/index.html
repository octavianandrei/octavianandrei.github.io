DevSecOps & Cloud Cost Optimization

I help teams incorporate security checks and cost awareness into everyday engineering. The aim is to make access, secrets, software delivery, and resource usage easier to review and maintain as the platform evolves.

When this work helps

Security and cost decisions often become harder to trace when they are handled separately from infrastructure and delivery. An engineering review connects configuration with ownership, examines the most useful controls, and identifies changes that can be verified without disrupting the workload.

What we can work on

  • Review identity, permissions, service accounts, and secret handling.
  • Introduce relevant dependency, image, and infrastructure checks into delivery workflows.
  • Improve environment boundaries and apply least privilege to deployment access.
  • Review resource utilization, idle environments, and cost allocation.
  • Define actionable guardrails and document the operational tradeoffs.

What the handover can include

A project can provide a prioritized findings list, configuration changes, pipeline checks, and guidance for ongoing review. Cost opportunities are evaluated against workload behavior and reliability requirements; savings depend on the actual environment.

A useful starting point

Bring your infrastructure configuration, delivery workflow, access model, billing overview, and the security or spending concern you want to address first.

We start by discussing your current setup, constraints, and intended outcome. From there, we agree on a focused assessment, a defined project, or ongoing engineering support.
